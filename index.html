How every project runs

  1. 1

    Find the gap

    What should people do differently — and is training really the fix?

  2. 2

    Map the actions

    The few decisions and tasks that matter on the job.

  3. 3

    Prototype early

    Working interactive drafts you can click through, not storyboards.

  4. 4

    Measure the change

    A baseline before day one and a check at day 30.

What I can design for you

For L&D teams, e-learning agencies and training providers who need an extra designer — for one module or a whole programme.

E-learning and blended programmes

From needs analysis to finished, tested material — built around realistic decisions rather than slides of content.

  • Scenario-based e-learning
  • Role-based learning journeys
  • Facilitator guides and job aids
  • Measurement plans

Practical AI workshops for teams

Hands-on sessions where people use AI tools on their own everyday work, with clear guidance on what not to share.

  • Tailored to each team's tasks

  • Attendance records if you need them

About Keithan

I've spent more than ten years helping adults learn at work: teaching Business English at Inlingua in Ingolstadt, then coaching executives on communication in China from 2022 to 2025.

Today I design learning experiences that start with a performance problem. I use AI tools throughout my own process — for rapid prototyping, scenario writing and building interactive artefacts — which is also what I teach teams to do.
